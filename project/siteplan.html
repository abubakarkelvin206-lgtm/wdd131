CyberSafe Guide

I chose the name CyberSafe Guide because it clearly describes a guide that helps people stay safe online.

Site purpose

CyberSafe Guide helps visitors learn the basics of cybersecurity and understand why staying safe online is important. It also teaches simple ways to protect personal information, devices, and accounts from cyber fraud and other online threats.

Scenarios

  • How can I protect my computer from being hacked?
  • How can I recognize a scam or fraudulent activity?

Color Scheme

Dark blue (#0B1F3A) will be used for the main header and major headings.

Light red (#E57373) will be used for the buttons and interactive accents

Typography

Poppins used for headings and site title

Roboto used for paragraph and general body text
