Stay Safe in a Connected World

Welcome to the CyberSafe Guide! Here you will find information and tips to help you stay safe online.

Why cybersecurity matters

Cybersecurity is important because it helps protect sensitive information, such as personal data and financial information, from being stolen or compromised by cybercriminals. It also helps prevent cyberattacks that can disrupt business operations and cause financial losses.

What you will learn

In this guide, you will learn about various aspects of cybersecurity, including common threats, best practices for staying safe online, and steps you can take to protect yourself and your information.

  • Recognize scams
  • Protect your personal information
  • Securing your devices
